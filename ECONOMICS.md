# Economics

**Unit Economics:**
- **Cost to run an audit:** ~$0.015 (Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet API call for the summary, plus negligible Vercel/Supabase edge computing costs).
- **Revenue per audit:** $0 (Free tool).
- **Monetization Strategy:** Lead generation for our core consulting firm (Credex) or a premium automated SaaS management platform.
- **Consulting LTV:** A successful enterprise optimization consult converts at $5,000 to $15,000 per engagement.

**Conversion Funnel Math:**
- Top of funnel: 10,000 visitors/month.
- Run audit (20% conversion): 2,000 audits/month.
- High-savings threshold detected (savings > $500/mo): ~15% of audits (300 leads).
- Book consultation (5% conversion from high-savings): 15 booked calls/month.
- Close deal (33% close rate): 5 new enterprise clients/month.

**Path to $1M ARR in 18 months:**
If the average consulting engagement yields $10k, 5 clients a month generates $50k/month ($600k ARR). 
To hit $1M ARR, we pivot from one-off consulting to a recurring SaaS model. We introduce a $99/mo premium tier that directly integrates with Okta, Google Workspace, and corporate credit cards to actively monitor and auto-downgrade inactive AI seats.
With 850 paying customers at $99/mo, we hit $1M ARR. At a target CAC of $150 (achieved through organic viral loops of the free audit tool), this requires approximately $125k in total acquisition spend, which is highly achievable within 18 months if the free tool acts as a loss-leader to aggregate the market.
