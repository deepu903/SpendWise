# Go-To-Market Strategy

**Target User:** Series A-C Startup Founders, Fractional CFOs, and VPs of Engineering. These individuals are responsible for managing burn rate and controlling decentralized software spend, which often spirals out of control as individual teams adopt different AI tools independently.

**Where they hang out:**
1. Private Slack communities (e.g., Fractional CFO networks, CTO Craft).
2. LinkedIn (actively posting about capital efficiency, runway, and team productivity).
3. Hacker News (when discussing tooling overkill and SaaS bloat).

**Getting the first 100 users in 30 days with $0 budget:**
1. **Cold Emailing Fractional CFOs:** Scrape LinkedIn for Fractional CFOs managing multiple startups. Send a highly targeted email: "I built a free tool that audits your clients' AI stack and surfaces $500+/mo in immediate savings. Takes 2 mins. Here's the link."
2. **Founder WhatsApp/Slack Groups:** Share the tool in YC/Techstars founder groups with a "show and tell" angle: "We realized we were burning $1k/mo on redundant AI tools. I built a quick script to audit it, turned it into a web app. Run your stack through it and post your savings."
3. **Engineering Blog Post / Hacker News:** Write a deeply technical but financially-focused article titled "How Startups are Quietly Burning $50k/Year on AI Tooling Bloat" and post it to Hacker News and Dev.to, linking back to SpendWise AI as the free solution.
4. **The Viral Loop:** The shareable results page (`/audit/[id]`) acts as a viral loop. When a CTO runs the audit, they share the link with their CEO/CFO to justify plan changes, pulling a new high-value decision-maker into the product.
